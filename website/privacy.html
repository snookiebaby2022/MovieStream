Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

What we collect

  • Account data: username, optional email, password hash
  • Preferences: wishlist, continue watching, profiles, language
  • Purchase status: Ad-Free flag and Stripe session id (no full card numbers stored by us)
  • Basic usage: page views / play events for admin analytics

How we use it

To run accounts, sync lists, unlock Ad-Free, improve reliability, and prevent abuse.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe. See Stripe’s privacy policy for payment data processing.

Third parties

We use TMDB for catalog metadata, Real-Debrid (when configured) for premium streams, and embed providers chosen at playback time. Those services have their own policies.

Cookies / local storage

We store login tokens and UI preferences in your browser (localStorage) so the site works across visits.

Retention & deletion

You can ask us to delete your account via Contact. Admin tools can also remove accounts.

Contact

Privacy questions: use the Contact form.
